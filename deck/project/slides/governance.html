Always-on agents have their own logins and act without you watching, so the account they run on matters. On personal plans, whether your data trains models depends on your settings; OpenAI says Business, Enterprise and Edu content is not used for training by default. In Enterprise, dots are in beta and off by default until a workspace admin enables them. Audit logs and action recording are Enterprise-only for Grok Bot, and reviewers note that its docs make no SOC 2, ISO 27001 or GDPR claims of xAI's own, so check before connecting customer data. Legally, in Australia the Privacy Act 1988 and the Australian Privacy Principles apply. Athlete health data is sensitive information and needs consent. APP 8 covers sending personal information overseas, which is exactly what a cloud computer may do. APP 11 requires you to secure it and destroy it when no longer needed. GDPR applies if you hold data on people in the EU.
