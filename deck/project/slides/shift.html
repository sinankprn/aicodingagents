Always-on agents

02 / 09

The landscape has changed

From answers to actions

Early LLMs could only give you text. Today's agents use tools to carry out the task itself.

Then · LLMs

text in, text out

Writes a draft email

Suggests a training plan

Explains how to book tickets

You copy, paste and do the work.

Now · Agents

goal in, work done

Sends it and books the meeting

Builds it and schedules the sessions

Books the tickets into your calendar

It uses the browser, apps and files for you.

The first wave of LLMs were brilliant writers but they could not touch anything. Every answer ended with you doing the work. Agents changed that: give them tools such as a browser, a terminal or an app connection and they can plan, act, check the result and keep going until the job is done. Always-on agents are the next step: the same capability, running continuously on a computer of their own.
