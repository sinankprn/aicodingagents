Muse from Meta, announced 8 September 2026: a personal agent connected to email, calendar, payments, health, shopping and smart home, with its own email address and, since 23 September, control of macOS apps. US only, free tier plus $20 and $100 plans. Grok Bot from xAI, early beta from 11 August 2026: always-on agents with their own persistent cloud computer, real browser, file system and terminal, and their own logins. Bundled with SuperGrok Heavy and Cursor Ultra and Teams Premium. dots from OpenAI, launched at DevDay on 29 September 2026: each dot has its own cloud computer and browser, reaches more than 4,000 apps, and you can message or call it in ChatGPT, Slack and Teams. Available on Pro, Business Premium and Enterprise. Sources: openai.com/index/introducing-dots, x.ai/news/introducing-grok-bot, Reuters and TechCrunch on Muse.
