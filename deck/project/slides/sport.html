Five places an always-on agent earns its keep in a sporting organisation. Performance: pull GPS, wellness and training load overnight and have a risk summary ready before the morning session. Ticketing: monitor sales by fixture, handle routine fan queries and flag games that need a push. Community sport: chase registrations, fill volunteer rosters and keep track of grant deadlines for clubs. Marketing: monitor campaign performance across channels every day and alert you when something underperforms. Commercial: research sponsors, qualify leads, draft outreach and keep the CRM up to date, asking before anything is sent. The pattern is the same each time: the agent does the legwork, a person approves anything external.
