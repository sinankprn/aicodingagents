Always-on agents

05 / 09

Definition

What are always-on agents?

An AI agent with a computer of its own that keeps working after you close the app.

Computer

A persistent cloud machine with a browser, files and a terminal

Logins

Signs into the tools you already use: email, Slack, CRM

Memory

Learns your preferences and standards from feedback

Schedule

Runs tasks on a timetable, day and night

Approvals

Checks back only when a decision needs you

Channels

Message or call it in chat, Slack, Teams or email

The defining feature is the computer. A chatbot lives inside your browser tab and stops when you close it. An always-on agent has its own persistent cloud machine with a real browser, file system and terminal, and its own logins to your tools. Add memory, a schedule and an approval model and you get something closer to a colleague than a chatbot: you hand it a job, it works through it end to end, and it only comes back when it needs a decision.
