Today

OpenClaw lives on as an open-source foundation

OpenClaw is the project that made always-on agents mainstream. Peter Steinberger launched it in November 2025 as Clawdbot; it was renamed Moltbot and then OpenClaw. You talk to it through ordinary messaging apps and it manages your calendar, books flights and more. It went viral, spawning Moltbook, a social network for AI agents. In February 2026 Steinberger joined OpenAI, and OpenClaw moved into a foundation as an open-source project that OpenAI continues to support. Sources: Wikipedia, TechCrunch (15 Feb 2026).
