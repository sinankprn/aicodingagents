Keeps you in control

The big idea is that the agent becomes an extension of you: your judgement and goals, with its time and stamina. From the launch material: dots and Grok Bot keep working 24/7 after you close the app, finish jobs end to end and only come back when something needs your approval. Memory means a dot learns your preferences from feedback. You can message them where you already work, in Slack, Teams or chat. Grok Bot lets several bots hand work off to each other. And dots' Custom Rules let you decide, per action, whether it acts on its own, acts if pre-approved, asks first or hands off to you.
